# Frontend

## Shell

- `GlobalShell` wraps the app in bootstrap, updater, toast, and modal-coordinator providers; `GlobalShellContent` in `src/layouts/GlobalShell.tsx` then renders the shell header, navigation rail, and route outlet.
- Header actions are split into community and application groups with a visual divider in `ShellHeaderActions` in `src/layouts/ShellHeader.tsx`. GitHub, X, Xiaohongshu, and Douyin share one icon-control treatment, while Bilibili and Support remain labelled controls, in `ShellSocialLinks` and `ShellSupportMenu` in `src/layouts/ShellHeader.tsx`.
- Bilibili and Support are controlled disclosures: Escape closes the open menu and restores its trigger, while outside pointer-down closes it, in the popover-dismissal effect inside `GlobalShellContent` (`src/layouts/GlobalShell.tsx`); their triggers expose expanded/control/menu relationships and their entries are menu items in `ShellSocialLinks` and `ShellSupportMenu` in `src/layouts/ShellHeader.tsx`.
- Windows custom chrome exposes minimize, maximize/restore, and close controls in `WindowsWindowControls` in `src/layouts/ShellHeader.tsx`. The maximize control initializes from native `isMaximized`, refreshes its localized tooltip/aria label and icon after window resize events, and invokes native toggle-maximize; while the stream service is running, close tooltip/aria switch to hide-to-tray copy via the shell-only poller `useShellStreamServiceRunning` in `src/features/stream/useShellStreamServiceRunning.ts`.
- At 920px and below, header community actions are hidden while application/window controls remain available, and the navigation rail and main padding also compact, in the `@media (max-width: 920px)` rules in `src/styles/index.css` (`.bpp-header-community-actions`, `.bpp-nav`, `.bpp-main-inner`). Product pages keep a shared large-window width cap through `.bpp-page` in `src/styles/index.css`, and the main content region owns low-height vertical scrolling through `.bpp-main` in `src/styles/index.css`.
- Payment and update dialogs register as global modal sources in `GlobalShellContent` (`src/layouts/GlobalShell.tsx`). Payment is informational; the updater presentation contract supplies the update modal's open state, priority, dismissal policy, and action, while manual update checking is exposed as an Install maintenance action in `Install` (`src/pages/Install.tsx`) and `InstallActionsPanel` (`src/features/install/InstallActionsPanel.tsx`).

## Copy And Locale

- `zh` is the source of truth for the message-key set and `en` is typed against it, so a missing or stale translation fails the build: the `zh` catalog, the `MessageKey` type (`keyof typeof zh`), and `en: Record<MessageKey, string>` all live in `src/i18n/messages.ts`. The catalog header fixes one word per concept — plugin, components, stream service, overlay — so surfaces cannot drift apart.
- Count-dependent English copy uses a `{name|singular|plural}` token resolved from the numeric parameter, which lets one sentence agree on several independent counts; `zh` omits the token. Resolution happens before plain `{name}` interpolation in `formatMessage` (using the `PLURAL_TOKEN` regex) in `src/i18n/messages.ts`.
- With no stored choice, the initial locale follows `navigator.languages` and only then falls back to `zh`, in `resolveInitialLocale` in `src/i18n/messages.ts`. `LocaleProvider` persists the choice, reflects it on `<html lang>`, and syncs the tray in `src/i18n/LocaleProvider.tsx`.
- Copy that names an OS surface selects a platform variant rather than assuming one host, through the shared user-agent check `isWindowsPlatform` in `src/features/shared/platform.ts`, consumed by `updaterProblemMessageKey` in `src/features/about/updaterProblems.ts` and `endGameProcessMessageKey` in `src/pages/History.tsx`.
- The small display labels are styled for Latin small-caps (Cinzel, uppercase, wide tracking) at 10-11px. Cinzel carries no CJK glyphs and uppercase is a no-op on Han characters, so under `zh` those labels reset their tracking and lift the smallest sizes, in the `html[lang^='zh']` CJK-label-typography rules in `src/styles/index.css`.

## Native-Feel Rules

- Product colors, surfaces, text roles, and radii have one semantic token source; Tailwind theme names alias those values instead of defining a second palette, in the `@theme` block and `:root` custom properties in `src/styles/index.css`. Shared panel, ordinary-button, segmented-selection, action-tile, and status-banner styling consumes those tokens via `.bpp-panel`, `.bpp-button`, `.bpp-segmented-control`, `.bpp-action-tile`, and `.bpp-status-banner` in `src/styles/index.css`; empty-state styling is `.bpp-empty-state` in `src/styles/index.css`.
- Ordinary button state and width-stable busy labels are implemented in `Button` in `src/components/ui/Button.tsx`; native radio semantics back segmented selection in `SegmentedControl` in `src/components/ui/SegmentedControl.tsx`; action tiles, live-region-aware status banners, and content-driven empty states live in `ActionTile` (`src/components/ui/ActionTile.tsx`), `StatusBanner` (`src/components/ui/StatusBanner.tsx`), and `EmptyState` (`src/components/ui/EmptyState.tsx`).
- Global app chrome disables page-style selection by default, keeps text controls selectable, and keeps focus-visible outlines, in the `@layer base` native-feel rules in `src/styles/index.css`.
- `.selectable` and `.user-content` opt content back into text selection in `src/styles/index.css`.
- Reduced motion is honored through the `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` rule in `src/styles/index.css`.
- App modals use the native `<dialog>` wrapper and top-layer dialog styling. `Dialog` in `src/components/ui/Dialog.tsx` consumes the active source's dismissal policy so Escape and backdrop cannot dismiss blocked work; dialog CSS is the `.bpp-dialog` selectors in `src/styles/index.css`.
- The coordinator displays exactly one registered dialog, prioritizes critical work over confirmations, system messages, and informational dialogs, and preserves FIFO within equal priorities, via `PRIORITY_RANK` and `createModalCoordinator` in `src/features/shared/modalCoordinator.ts`. Unmounting a queued route source is safe (`unregister` inside `createModalCoordinator`); after the queue drains, focus returns to the connected trigger or the current page heading/main fallback via `restoreModalFocus` in `src/features/shared/modalCoordinator.ts`, the focus-restore effect in `ModalCoordinatorProvider` in `src/components/ui/ModalCoordinator.tsx`, and the `Title` heading (`data-page-heading`) in `src/components/ui/PageHeader.tsx`.
- Install, reset, uninstall, cleanup, and video-delete confirmations share the `Dialog`-composing `ConfirmDialog` in `src/components/ui/ConfirmDialog.tsx`. It requires an explicit `ActiveDismissalPolicy` for Escape, backdrop, close, and secondary actions; blocked work removes the secondary cancel affordance, disables close, says that the operation cannot be cancelled, and reserves both idle/busy labels so loading does not resize the action, in `ConfirmDialog` and `requestConfirmDialogDismiss` in `src/components/ui/ConfirmDialog.tsx`. Their modal sources begin as confirmations and adopt critical blocked semantics for native work because none exposes a cancellation contract (the `ModalSource` usages in `Install` in `src/pages/Install.tsx`, `StorageCleanupCard` in `src/features/history/StorageCleanupCard.tsx`, and `RunDetail` in `src/pages/RunDetail.tsx`).
- The current Tauri security config sets `security.csp` to `null` in `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json`; treat any CSP hardening claim as future work until code changes.

## Runtime Seam

- `commandClient` selects the normalized generated native client or Browser Preview adapter once at module load in `src/api/commandClient.ts`; feature APIs call typed command functions rather than command strings.
- Both adapters implement the `CommandAdapter` contract derived from the generated command object in `src/api/commandAdapter.ts`. The native adapter normalizes backend rejections via `normalizeBackendError`/`withNormalizedErrors` in `src/api/nativeCommands.ts` and preserves validated semantic problems via `isSemanticProblem` in `src/api/problems.ts`, while Preview declares every generated operation and returns scope-tagged cleanup values for both scopes in `createPreviewCommands` in `src/api/previewCommands.ts`.
- Shared install, stream, crop, history, cleanup, and bootstrap preview values live in the leaf module `src/api/previewDefaults.ts`; Preview reuses those object references in `createPreviewCommands` (`src/api/previewCommands.ts`) so polling preserves React state bailouts.
- Stream commands pass through one semantic port over the selected native or Preview command adapter in `createStreamCommandPort` in `src/features/stream/streamApi.ts`. The framework-neutral workflow owns replayable initialization, polling freshness and response epochs, capability-scoped operations/problems, semantic notices, and the derived page snapshot in `DefaultStreamWorkflow` (methods `start`, `poll`, `restart`, and others) and its `deriveSnapshot` method in `src/features/stream/streamWorkflow.ts`; `useStreamPage` supplies browser ports and creates the workflow once, independently of locale, before binding its lifecycle to React in `src/features/stream/useStreamPage.ts`.
- Install commands pass through one semantic port over the selected native or Preview command adapter in `createInstallCommandPort` in `src/features/install/installApi.ts`. The framework-neutral Install workflow owns authoritative `InstallState`, single-flight operations, target-bearing confirmations, fixed-parameter retry, failure reconciliation, semantic notices, and the derived page snapshot in `DefaultInstallWorkflow` (its `start`/`refresh`/`chooseDirectory`/`confirm` methods and others, and its `deriveSnapshot` method) in `src/features/install/installWorkflow.ts`; `useInstallPage` creates the workflow once and only binds start/dispose plus external-store subscription in `src/features/install/useInstallPage.ts`.
- The shared page-state seam is a discriminated union of initial loading, blocking failure, ready-empty, and ready-content with nested idle/refreshing/failed refresh state; request ids reject stale completions via `PageState`, `completePageRequest`, and `failPageRequest` in `src/features/shared/pageState.ts`. Shared UI problems retain code, parameters, and optional diagnostics separately from localized copy via the `UiProblem` type in `src/features/shared/problems.ts`; `ProblemBanner` in `src/components/ui/ProblemBanner.tsx` preserves localized message/actions/diagnostics while delegating live-region structure to `StatusBanner`.
- About bootstrap uses its own discriminated resource snapshot because packaged data can remain usable while native provenance fails. The `AppBootstrapSnapshot` type labels unavailable fallback fields, and `createAppBootstrapMachine` keeps retry single-flight and replaces fallback in place after recovery, both in `src/features/about/appBootstrap.ts`; React creates it once and exposes the packaged fallback to existing shell consumers in `useAppBootstrapState` in `src/features/about/useAppBootstrap.ts`.
- Destructive workflows use a framework-neutral confirmed-operation controller that keeps the target across confirming/running/failure, allows pending-target updates while confirming, rejects conflicting requests and repeat execution, blocks dismissal while running, closes only after success, and retains a semantic problem for retry or safe exit after failure, in `createConfirmedOperationController` in `src/features/shared/confirmedOperation.ts`.
- Run Detail specializes that seam with a distinct not-found state, preserved ready content on refresh failure, and a separate action state that globally gates conflicting work while retaining target-scoped failures, via the `RunDetailPageState`/`reduceRunDetailPageState` and `RunDetailActionState` types in `src/features/history/runDetailPageState.ts`. Its semantic problem presenter maps stable backend codes and operation parameters to localized copy without using diagnostics as user-facing text, via `runDetailProblemMessageKey`/`presentRunDetailProblem` in `src/features/history/runDetailProblems.ts`.

## Current Product Surfaces

- Install renders mutually exclusive initial-detection, blocking-failure, and completed-state branches from the workflow snapshot; a refresh failure keeps the completed status and actions visible behind a localized retry banner, in `Install` (`src/pages/Install.tsx`) reading `DefaultInstallWorkflow.deriveSnapshot` (`src/features/install/installWorkflow.ts`).
- Install's primary surface is a status hero with one choose-directory/install/repair/launch action, followed by selected-directory and application-version cards and a shared warning banner, in `InstallStatusPanel` in `src/features/install/InstallStatusPanel.tsx`. The four shared action tiles are delete local data (danger), reset BepInEx, check for updates, and uninstall (danger) in `InstallActionsPanel` in `src/features/install/InstallActionsPanel.tsx`.
- Install renders exactly one primary action from the workflow snapshot. Mode and disabled/running state are derived with native action gates in `deriveInstallPrimaryAction` in `src/features/install/installWorkflow.ts` and consumed by the large primary `Button` in `PrimaryInstallActionButton` in `src/features/install/PrimaryInstallActionButton.tsx`.
- Install warnings, failures, and success notices are presented from stable semantic codes in bilingual frontend copy; native diagnostics stay in the diagnostic disclosure rather than the message, via `presentInstallWarning`/`presentInstallProblem`/`presentInstallNotice` in `src/features/install/installProblems.ts` and `InstallProblemBanner` in `src/features/install/InstallProblemBanner.tsx`. The route translates notice codes once and acknowledges their ids so toasts are not duplicated by rerenders, in the notice-toast effect in `Install` (`src/pages/Install.tsx`).
- The delete-local-data tile is danger-marked and disabled unless workflow availability allows reset data; its title and detail switch to a no-data message when the game path is valid but no resettable data exists, in `InstallActionsPanel` in `src/features/install/InstallActionsPanel.tsx`. Uninstall is also danger-marked and opens a lightweight confirmation (no acknowledgement checkbox) before native work, in `InstallActionsPanel` (`src/features/install/InstallActionsPanel.tsx`) and `UninstallConfirmModal` (`src/features/install/UninstallConfirmModal.tsx`). Install, both delete/reset, and uninstall confirmations retain fixed targets through failure; acknowledgement checkboxes (install and destructive resets only) remain UI-only and reset when confirmation opens or closes, while success alone closes the confirmation, in `Install` (`src/pages/Install.tsx`).
- Cleanup failures are localized from semantic problem codes/operation parameters while diagnostics remain separate, via `presentStorageCleanupProblem`/`storageCleanupProblemMessageKey` in `src/features/history/storageCleanupProblems.ts`. The History and Run Detail surfaces those problems appear on are described in `docs/truth/history-stream.md`.
- Stream's single panel separates service status/actions, labelled OBS output with guide text and Host/Port/DB/Window metrics, then native-radio display mode and crop actions, all within `Stream` in `src/pages/Stream.tsx`. Capability problems and notices surface as toasts with optional recovery actions rather than inline banners, via `useStreamProblemToast`/`useStreamNoticeToast` in `src/pages/Stream.tsx`.
- Stream status, database, window, and notice copy is derived from the current translator at render time. Stale running/stopped values have distinct presentation and are not presented as authoritative, in `presentStreamSnapshot` in `src/features/stream/streamPresentation.ts`.
- About renders selectable app/BPP version values, a GitHub link, and Credits with distinct Contributors/Acknowledgements subgroup headings, in `AboutBootstrapContent` in `src/pages/About.tsx`. Initial/fallback/blocking feedback uses shared live-region banners, a width-stable native retry control, bilingual semantic copy, and optional diagnostics; fallback states identify packaged data in the banner instead of showing implementation provenance during normal operation, in `AboutBootstrapFeedback` in `src/pages/About.tsx`.

## Update Modal

- The updater presentation contract derives phase metadata plus modal title/action/priority/dismissal for every phase. Available, ready, and recovery decisions are system/dismissible; downloading, installing, and restarting are critical/blocked and have no fake cancel action, in `getUpdaterUiContract` in `src/features/about/updaterPresentation.ts` and `ShellUpdateModal` in `src/layouts/ShellUpdateModal.tsx`. The manual check entry point is the Install maintenance action in `InstallActionsPanel` in `src/features/install/InstallActionsPanel.tsx`.
- Progress exposes an accessible label, minimum, known maximum/value, value text, and polite status announcement; unknown totals stay indeterminate rather than inventing a maximum, in `UpdateDownloadProgress` in `src/layouts/ShellUpdateModal.tsx`.
- Check, download, install, and restart failures use stable semantic codes with localized retry guidance and optional diagnostic disclosure, via `updaterProblemFromError`/`presentUpdaterProblem`/`updaterProblemMessageKey` in `src/features/about/updaterProblems.ts`. Restart failure preserves the installed version, explains reopening the app from Applications, and offers another restart attempt, via `restart`/`canRestart` in `createUpdaterMachine` (`src/features/about/updater.ts`) and the failed-phase branch of `ShellUpdateModal` (`src/layouts/ShellUpdateModal.tsx`).
